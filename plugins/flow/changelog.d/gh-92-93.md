bump: minor

- `steward.sh reserve [--name <who>]` takes a free pool worktree for the owner's manual work: a
  claim with no item under `<who>` (default: git `user.name`, else `$USER`), under the pick lock,
  path printed. With none free it grows the pool or exits 3 at `pool_max`, as `claim` does; a bad
  option exits 2. `claim`, `sweep`, `health` (no fast-forward or move of a free worktree, no
  empty-claim release) and the `next` job pass over it; `release <path>` frees it as any claim.
  `pool` and `board` show it as reserved by that name.
- `steward.sh sweep` says `<item>: cleared the merge gate` when it clears one, so the console's
  Sweep button and the job logs show which gates went with the worktrees.
